01

Introduction

This draft privacy notice has been prepared for HAXOTECH LIMITED to explain the handling of personal information associated with its corporate website and business communications.

HAXOTECH LIMITED provides mobile application development, custom software development, website development, systems integration, and IT consulting and support.

The organization must review and approve this notice against its actual information handling practices before adopting it as an effective privacy policy.

02

Scope of This Policy

This notice is intended to cover visitors to the corporate website associated with haxotechlimited.online and individuals who communicate with the organization through its business contact channels.

Separate mobile applications, software products, client information systems and third-party services may require additional privacy disclosures and contractual arrangements.

03

Information Collected

The current corporate website is designed as an informational resource and does not contain user registration forms, payment forms or account login functionality.

Information associated with website hosting and business communications may include:

  • Names and email addresses voluntarily provided in correspondence.
  • Business enquiries and technical support requests.
  • Information supplied when discussing software and technology services.
  • IP addresses and technical connection information processed by hosting infrastructure.
  • Browser information, device information and website request logs processed by service providers.

Individuals should avoid sending passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information through general email.

04

How Information May Be Used

Subject to the organization's confirmed information handling arrangements, relevant information may be processed to:

  • Respond to business enquiries.
  • Discuss software and technology requirements.
  • Provide requested information and technical assistance.
  • Maintain website availability and security.
  • Investigate technical problems.
  • Communicate regarding business arrangements.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
05

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection legislation requires a legal basis for processing personal information, relevant grounds may include:

  • Consent, where required and freely provided.
  • Performance of a contract or steps requested before entering a contract.
  • Legitimate interests, subject to applicable individual rights.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.

The organization must determine the applicable legal grounds according to its jurisdiction and actual processing activities.

06

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The current corporate website source does not intentionally install advertising cookies, analytics cookies or tracking scripts.

Hosting providers, browser services and external websites may independently process technical information under their own privacy arrangements.

If advertising, analytics or other tracking technologies are introduced, the relevant privacy disclosures and consent arrangements must be reviewed and updated.

07

Information Sharing and Disclosure

Personal information may be processed by service providers supporting website hosting, email communications and technical operations.

Information may also require disclosure for purposes such as:

  • Responding to requested services or enquiries.
  • Maintaining information system security.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.
  • Responding to valid requests from competent authorities.
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal rights.

Actual recipients and sharing arrangements must be confirmed before this notice is finalized.

08

Data Retention

Retention periods should reflect the purposes for which information is collected, the nature of relevant business relationships and applicable legal requirements.

Hosting and email providers may retain operational records and backups according to their own retention policies.

The organization's specific retention periods and information deletion procedures remain subject to confirmation.

09

Data Security

Appropriate technical and organizational security measures are important for protecting personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

HTTPS encryption is planned for the corporate website through its hosting infrastructure. The final security configuration must be tested before launch.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Individuals should exercise appropriate care when sharing information online.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws and individual circumstances, individuals may have rights including:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally applicable.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing activities.
  • Requesting data portability where applicable.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Submitting complaints to a competent data protection authority.

The organization must confirm the procedures for responding to privacy requests, applicable deadlines and relevant supervisory authorities.

11

Children's Privacy

The corporate website is designed to provide general business and technology information. It does not contain child-specific registration or account creation functionality.

Any applications or software services intended for younger audiences must have appropriate privacy arrangements reflecting their intended users and applicable legal requirements.

12

Third-Party Services and Links

The corporate website is being prepared for hosting through GitHub Pages. Business email services are planned through Namecheap Private Email.

These providers may process information in connection with their respective services. Further information is available in their privacy documentation:

  • GitHub General Privacy Statement
  • Namecheap Privacy Policy

External websites and services may operate independently and have their own privacy policies and terms.

13

International Data Processing

Website hosting, communications and supporting infrastructure providers may operate in different countries.

Consequently, information associated with website visits and business communications may be processed outside an individual's country of residence.

Relevant transfer arrangements, safeguards and applicable legal requirements must be reviewed by the organization before adopting this policy.

14

Mobile Applications and Software

HAXOTECH LIMITED's confirmed business activities include mobile application development and custom software development.

Individual applications and software products require privacy disclosures reflecting their actual functionality and information handling practices.

Where applicable, separate application privacy policies must address:

  • Personal information and device information collected.
  • Application permissions and their purposes.
  • Advertising and analytics technologies.
  • Third-party software development kits and service providers.
  • Information sharing and retention practices.
  • Data security arrangements.
  • Account deletion and personal data deletion procedures.
  • Applicable children's privacy requirements.

This corporate website privacy notice does not replace the privacy policy required for any individual mobile application or software service.

15

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This notice should be reviewed whenever website functionality, service providers, information handling arrangements or applicable legal requirements change.

Following organization approval, the effective policy and subsequent revisions should be published on this page with the appropriate effective or update date.
